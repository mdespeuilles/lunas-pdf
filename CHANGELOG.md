# Lunas PDF changelog

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-04

- Fixed a crash when opening a PDF from Finder on macOS.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-04

- Fixed an issue that prevented the app from launching on macOS.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-04

- **New name and icon:** the app is now called Lunas PDF and has a new page-and-pencil icon.
- **PDF reading and annotation:** view PDFs and annotate them.
- **Text boxes:** a text box now starts exactly where you click, even near the right edge of the page, and begins one line tall.
- **Moving annotations:** open-hand and closed-hand cursors show when you can move an annotation and when you are moving it.
- **Mini toolbar:** the toolbar now stays inside the page near the right edge.
- **Single-use tools:** placement tools now switch off after one use.
- **Copy, cut and paste annotations:** use Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V.
- **Checkboxes:** checkboxes are black by default.
- **Redaction:** the black box appears as soon as you place a redaction.
- **Forms:** fill in AcroForm forms.
- **Digital signatures:** sign documents and verify signatures against the EU and Microsoft trust lists.
- **Organize pages:** rearrange the pages of a document, with a prompt to use side-by-side view.
- **Export and print:** export and print documents, with native printing on macOS.
- **AI assistant:** a new panel lets you ask questions about the document and run actions on it.
- **Home tab:** the "+" button and Ctrl+T now open the Home tab.
- **Omarchy theme:** the app can follow the Omarchy theme.
- **Window buttons:** macOS uses its native buttons on the left, and other platforms use flat buttons.
- **Automatic updates:** the app now updates itself to new versions.
- **Startup:** the app no longer runs without a window, and it prints a step-by-step startup log in the terminal.
